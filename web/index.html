⚔️ About the Game

You are an eternal challenger in Eidolon Castle, a living fortress corrupted by ancient magic. The castle adapts relentlessly through the Castle Pressure Index (CPI) — a measure of how much your power threatens its dominion.

🏰 No Checkpoints

Death returns you to the entrance. Every run counts.

📈 Permanent Escalation

CPI never resets. The castle grows stronger forever.

⚡ Deep Combat

Light, balanced, heavy, ranged, and hybrid weapons.

🎯 Adaptive AI

Enemies mutate and evolve with rising CPI.

🎮 Controls

A / D Move Left / Right
W / Space Jump
Shift Dodge Roll
Left Click Attack
Right Click Block
E Interact
